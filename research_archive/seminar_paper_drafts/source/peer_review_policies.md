# Journal-specific peer-review policy audit

**Audit date:** 2026-10-09  
**Purpose:** Verify the publication-process evidence for the strict literature set using primary journal or publisher pages. This is a source-screening memo, not a claim that every article in a journal is relevant or that a particular article’s historical review file is available.

## Decision rule

`PASS` means that a journal-specific primary page explicitly identifies peer review, referees, reviewers, or a review model beyond editorial screening. The user’s gate is peer-reviewed literature; the policy need not use the literal word “external.” `UNKNOWN` means that no journal-specific peer-review evidence was verified or the relevant page could not be retrieved. Generic Elsevier ethics language was not used as evidence for an Elsevier journal.

All quoted text below is short (no more than 25 words from any one source). The policy check concerns the literature gate; provenance for the Duflo–Hanna–Ryan replication data is a separate issue.

## Exclusion note

**Journal of Economic Perspectives (JEP)** remains excluded. The relevant item is a solicited/editor-reviewed publication type, and this audit does not upgrade it without article-type-specific peer-review evidence. Do not use a general publisher policy to upgrade it.

## Related source-article journal

The reference study, Duflo, Hanna, and Ryan (2012), was published in the *American Economic Review*. The AEA’s [AER Editorial Policy](https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/aer/editorial-policy) states: “All manuscripts submitted to the American Economic Review undergo single-blind peer review.” The policy also preserves the editors’ right to return a manuscript without referee review. This verifies the journal’s current peer-review process; it does not provide the historical referee reports or article-level review record for the 2012 paper.

## Method citations checked

- Cameron, Gelbach, and Miller (2008) is cited in the main paper only for the general cluster-bootstrap inference context. The official [MIT Press article page](https://direct.mit.edu/rest/article/90/3/414/57731/Bootstrap-Based-Improvements-for-Inference-with) describes cluster bootstrap-t procedures for inference with few clusters. The paper does not attribute the draft’s seed, draw count, or exact null-imposed implementation to Cameron et al.
- Holm (1979) is cited only for the multiple-testing adjustment. The [JSTOR record](https://www.jstor.org/stable/4615733) identifies the *Scandinavian Journal of Statistics* article; the method is the sequentially rejective procedure used here to adjust the fixed outcome family. No broader empirical claim is drawn from Holm (1979).

## Practical use

Use only the `PASS` rows for the peer-reviewed-only literature set. For each retained citation, separately verify its bibliographic record and article type; a journal-wide policy does not prove that an individual article’s historical referee reports are available. Keep the article-specific limitation explicit when describing the source article or older methods references.
