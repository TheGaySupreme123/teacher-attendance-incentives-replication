# Research archive

This folder preserves the substantive work that led to Shay Ben Shabtay's final paper: analysis scripts, saved results, exploratory candidate comparisons, literature summaries, example reference drafts, alternative approaches, copyediting materials, and seminar notes.

**The current paper is [`../paper/BenShabtay_Paper.pdf`](../paper/BenShabtay_Paper.pdf). Reproduce its main results with [`../replication/`](../replication/).** Files here are historical, and earlier sample choices, descriptions, page counts, AI statements, or uses of the word “final” may have been superseded. The archive does not upgrade earlier exploratory findings to confirmatory or preregistered findings.

- `scripts/` and `outputs/` retain the broader research sequence, including alternatives that were not selected for the final paper. Saved outputs preserve their original scope and caveats. The main replication package certifies the stated 12 numerical checks, not every analysis in this folder.
- `notes/` contains the original research memos and work plan.
- `seminar_materials/` contains seminar talking points and slide-text planning.
- `seminar_paper_drafts/` contains own example/reference papers, separate alternatives, the peer-reviewed literature guide and its source-screening notes, editing records, and document builders. Earlier drafts are for development/reference, not additional submitted papers.
- `final_formatting/` records the final authoring builder/configuration and its stated limits.

The archive retains the original relative directory structure for inspection. Its analysis scripts use `data/` under their project root. To rerun an archived candidate, work in a separate copy, obtain the complete original release from https://doi.org/10.3886/E112523V1 if it needs files beyond the curated package, and provide that `data/` folder at the archive root. Some early plotting scripts also require `seaborn`, and document builders require their original authoring dependencies; these historical tools are outside the validated quick reproduction command. Original Stata programs and the structural model were not run for this paper.

Original article downloads and full extracted article text, course handout copies/extracts, browser/session logs, full AI conversations, temporary exports, caches, and duplicate page-render screenshots are omitted. Bibliographic citations and short attributed quotations remain in the own research materials. Local workspace paths in historical metadata describe the original run and are not access credentials.

The original data/program license and attribution are preserved in [`../replication/DATA_PROVENANCE.md`](../replication/DATA_PROVENANCE.md) and [`../replication/LICENSE-original.txt`](../replication/LICENSE-original.txt).
